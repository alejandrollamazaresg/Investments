Analytical Approach

I
Industry & Business Analysis

Each report starts with the sector landscape, competitive positioning, and business model — the context needed before touching any numbers.

II
Financial Modeling

A bottom-up forecast built from the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flows. Key drivers are identified and modeled individually rather than extrapolated from headline growth.

III
DCF Valuation

A discounted cash flow model anchors the price target. Public comparables are used as a cross-check, with peer groups filtered for actual comparability.

IV
Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis

Each report includes a sensitivity matrix across WACC, terminal growth, and revenue assumptions so that bull and bear cases are visible side by side.
