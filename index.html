Analytical Approach

I
Fundamental First

Every thesis begins with a deep understanding of the business model, competitive position, and long-term industry dynamics before a single multiple is applied.

II
DCF Primary

Discounted cash flow analysis is the primary valuation tool. Comparables serve as a sanity check, not the foundation—peer groups are scrutinised for comparability.

III
Conservative Assumptions

Growth assumptions are deliberately kept below recent historical rates. Conviction in a thesis should not require heroic forecasts to generate compelling returns.

IV
Scenario Discipline

Each report includes a full sensitivity matrix across WACC, terminal growth, and top-line assumptions, making bull and bear cases explicit and quantifiable.
